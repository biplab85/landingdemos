Our story

A Kitchen-Table Ritual that Grew into a Brand

Dewra began with a jar of homemade oat balm, mixed for a sister’s sensitive skin and shared with friends who kept asking for more.

Today every formula is still made in small batches in our studio, using cold-pressed oils, plant ceramides and soothing botanicals — never fillers, never harsh fragrance.

We test with dermatologists, package in recyclable glass and keep our ingredient lists short, so you always know exactly what you are putting on your skin.
