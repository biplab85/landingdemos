Commonly Asked Queries About Services.

Answer to the Question

We prepare and lodge tax returns for individuals, companies and trusts, handle BAS and GST, cloud bookkeeping, payroll and super, business structuring, SMSF administration and virtual CFO advisory — everything under one roof.

Fixed fees agreed upfront, one named accountant who knows your file, replies within one business day and proactive planning — we flag opportunities before tax time, not after.

It depends on your plan and needs. Most business clients meet quarterly, advisory clients monthly, and individuals once a year before lodgement — with your accountant only a message away in between.

Our team includes Chartered Accountants (CA ANZ), CPAs and registered tax agents, with specialists in SMSF and property. Everyone completes ongoing professional development every year.

Once we have your records, most individual returns are prepared within a few business days and lodged as soon as you approve them online. Business and trust returns usually take one to two weeks.
