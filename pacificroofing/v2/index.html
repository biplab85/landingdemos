Our services

Roofing, done properly.

From a single cracked tile to full gutter replacement — a comprehensive range of roofing, guttering and maintenance work for domestic and commercial properties.

01

Roof repairs

We replace all cracked and broken tiles, and inspect every ridge capping — reporting any that are loose, broken or in need of rebed and pointing.

02

Valley replacement

Rusted, poorly maintained valleys are a common cause of leaks. We replace them with new galvanised or Colorbond valleys, so water flows where it should.

03

Gutter repairs

New Colorbond gutters and downpipes in a wide range of colours to suit any home — downpipes in Colorbond or PVC, whichever you prefer.

04

Insurance & strata

We service strata and real-estate managed properties, looking after owners and occupants personally as well as technically. Insurance work welcome.

05

Rebed & re-point ridge caps

We bed ridge caps on fresh cement and re-point with flexible pointing — adding years of life while moving naturally with the house.

06

Gutter cleaning

Blocked gutters flood eaves and ceilings. We clean every gutter type and unblock downpipes — with optional 3, 6 or 12-month regular cleans.
