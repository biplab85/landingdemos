# Company-Specific Requirements: Sklentr

Company website: https://sklentr.com/

Portfolio website: https://www.sklentr.com/portfolio/

## Brand Identity and Business Context

Build the virtual office experience specifically for Sklentr, a digital agency that brings together website design, mobile app and MVP development, digital marketing, paid advertising, video editing, and business consultation.

Use the official company website as the primary source for company-approved branding, service descriptions, portfolio content, and business information.

Do not invent company details, employee identities, achievements, client endorsements, or project results.

## Office Zones

Create a connected virtual office with the following dedicated areas:

1. **Reception and Welcome Area:** Display the Sklentr brand identity and introduce the visitor to the virtual office tour.
2. **Development Studio:** Showcase website development, mobile applications, MVP development, and digital product engineering.
3. **Design Studio:** Present UI/UX design workflows, interface concepts, design reviews, and collaboration between designers and developers.
4. **Digital Marketing Studio:** Showcase SEO, social media, content strategy, paid advertising, and campaign planning.
5. **Creative Production Studio:** Feature video editing, promotional videos, social content, and product demonstrations.
6. **Client Consultation Room:** Demonstrate project discovery, business consultation, scope planning, and client discussions.
7. **Portfolio Gallery:** Display real Sklentr projects with interactive case studies.
8. **Technology Showcase:** Present relevant technologies used by the company, based on its current website.
9. **Project Planning Room:** Illustrate Sklentr's workflow through discovery, planning, creation, and review.
10. **Contact and Consultation Area:** Provide an interactive way to explore the next steps for working with Sklentr.

## Actual Services

Populate the interactive services wall using the service categories published on Sklentr's website:

- Website Design
- Mobile Apps & MVPs
- SEO & Marketing
- Paid Ads
- Video Editing or Video Production, according to the current service presentation
- Business Consultation

Each service must have its own interactive detail panel, relevant visuals, explanatory content, and links to related projects when available.

## Real Portfolio Integration

Use actual portfolio content from Sklentr's portfolio website rather than fictional project examples.

Prioritize projects such as:

- KindredCare
- GAinData
- Agile Sourcing
- AI Farming
- Get Takaful
- RoboReg
- ByTheLook
- Popup2gether
- MedTouch
- Medoptix
- KoutuRemix
- Navigotech

Verify each project's current status, description, and publicly available media before including it.

Each portfolio display should open a detailed case study with the project's actual description, challenges, solutions, technologies, screenshots, and verified outcomes where available.

Do not imply that every project is currently live or that every reported outcome is independently verified.

## Company Workflow Experience

Create a dedicated interactive workflow display illustrating these four stages:

**Discover → Plan → Create → Review**

When the visitor selects a stage, open an information panel explaining the process and how it contributes to delivering the agreed project scope.

## Virtual Employee Conversations

Create fictional but professionally appropriate virtual employee roles unless actual staff information and approved representations are supplied.

Provide contextual dialogue covering:

- Welcome to Sklentr.
- An introduction to the company's services.
- How the team approaches website and application projects.
- How the company plans and delivers MVPs.
- How digital marketing and creative services support business goals.
- How prospective clients can discuss their projects.

All dialogue should be based on approved company information. Avoid presenting fictional conversations as recordings of real Sklentr employees.

## Final Experience

The completed experience should communicate Sklentr's identity as a digital agency combining development, design, marketing, creative production, and business consultation.

The virtual office must function as an interactive company presentation, a portfolio showcase, and a guided introduction to Sklentr's services.

Keep the Unreal Engine architecture modular and data-driven so the company can update its services, portfolio, dialogue, and branding without rebuilding the entire 3D environment.
