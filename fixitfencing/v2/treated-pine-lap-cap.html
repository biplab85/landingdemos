Overview

Lap and cap fencing is one of the most popular residential fencing styles in Sydney, and it's easy to see why. Each paling overlaps the next, creating a solid fence with no gaps — giving you full privacy while delivering a clean, attractive look from both sides.

The horizontal capping rail along the top of the fence adds a finished, professional appearance and helps protect the top of the palings from weather damage. It’s a small detail that makes a big difference to the overall look and lifespan of the fence.

FixIt Fencing uses quality treated pine for our lap and cap installations, ensuring your fence is protected against termites, rot, and decay.
