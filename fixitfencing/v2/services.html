What we build

Fencing & retaining wall services

A complete range of residential and commercial fencing solutions, built to last by a team with over 15 years of hands-on experience.

FixIt Fencing offers a full suite of fencing and retaining wall services across Sydney's Hills District, Lower North Shore, Western Sydney, Hawkesbury Region, and Parramatta. Whether you're looking for the strength of Colorbond steel, the natural warmth of hardwood timber, or the modern look of aluminium slats, we have the expertise and materials to deliver.
