Pipe relining

The “no-dig” repair that lasts 50 years

Traditional pipe replacement is destructive — tearing up driveways, gardens and slabs. Relining cures a new pipe inside the old one, giving you a structurally sound, full-bore solution backed by a 50-year guarantee, without the excavation.

  • No excavation, no landscaping damage
  • 50-year manufacturer-backed guarantee
  • Resistant to tree roots, corrosion & cracking
  • Finished in a fraction of dig-and-replace time
