Our philosophy

Slow dough, open fire & the patience of tradition

Every loaf, every pizza base and every sheet of pasta begins the same way — with stone-ground flour, natural leavening and two full days of fermentation. It's slower. It's also the only way we know to make food that tastes this alive.

  • 48-hour fermented doughLight, blistered and easy to digest.
  • Sonoma farm producePicked this week, cooked tonight.

Frequently asked questions

From reservations to dietary needs — everything you need before dining with us.

Still have a question? Call (415) 555-0142

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking ahead on Thursday to Saturday evenings and for groups of five or more to avoid a wait.

Monday to Thursday 12:00 – 10:00 PM, Friday and Saturday 12:00 – 11:00 PM, and Sunday 12:00 – 9:00 PM. The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before close.

Yes. Around a third of the menu is vegetarian, we have dedicated vegan dishes, and any pizza or pasta can be made with our gluten-free dough. Let your server know about allergies and the kitchen will take care of you.

Order on this site for pickup in about 20 minutes, or for delivery within 3 miles of Market Street. Pizzas travel in vented boxes so the crust stays crisp.

Absolutely. Our private room seats up to 24, and full buy-outs host up to 120. We also cater weddings and off-site events for up to 200 guests.

The dining room and restrooms are fully step-free. Validated parking is available at the Fifth & Mission garage, two minutes' walk away, and Powell St BART is around the corner.
