About Sklentr

Our Story

Sklentr began in 2012 as a twelve-seat counter around a single wood oven. Today the oven still sets the rhythm of the kitchen — every dough rests for 48 hours, every sauce is made in-house and every plate leaves the pass looking its best.

We buy whole from growers, fishers and farmers within a hundred miles, then let the seasons write the menu. No shortcuts, no freezers, no fuss — just honest Italian cooking and a room full of people who love to eat.
