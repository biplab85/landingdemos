FAQs

Frequently asked questions

We advise on family and estates, criminal defence, commercial and corporate matters, property, employment and civil litigation.

If you are facing a dispute, signing a significant agreement or unsure of your rights, early advice can save time, money and stress. Even one consultation helps you understand where you stand.

We talk through what has happened, clarify the key facts and outline your realistic options, likely costs and the next steps.

Bring contracts, letters, emails, court papers, photos or anything else connected to your matter. A short timeline of events is also very helpful.

Timeframes depend on the complexity of the issue and how the other side responds. Once we review your file we will give you an honest estimate and keep it updated.

Many services are offered at a fixed fee. Complex matters use transparent hourly rates, always agreed in writing before any work begins.
